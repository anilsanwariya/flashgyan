# Rajasthan: districts and divisions

Use these names, in these spellings, for every place's district. A place's district is always today's (41 districts, since Dec 2024). A fact about an older map says so: "(33-district map)" or "(2023, 50 districts)".

## The three maps

- 33 districts: 2008 (Pratapgarh, the 33rd) to 6 Aug 2023.
- 50 districts and 10 divisions: 7 Aug 2023 to Dec 2024.
- 41 districts and 7 divisions: since Dec 2024 (today).
